## 🎯 Featured Projects

### 🧠 AI & Machine Learning

- **Detecting AI-Generated Product Reviews**  
  Comparative research using traditional ML and Transformer-based models to distinguish authentic product reviews from AI-generated reviews.

- **BinBot – AI Waste Classification**  
  Computer vision system using **YOLOv8 and MobileNetV2** to identify and classify different categories of waste.

- **CareerCompass AI**  
  Multi-agent AI career platform featuring skill-gap analysis, learning-path generation, interview simulation, job matching, and AI orchestration.

### 📊 Data Science & Analytics

- **Flood Risk Analysis Dashboard**  
  Data-driven flood-risk analysis using geographic, environmental, rainfall, and infrastructure indicators with interactive Power BI visualizations.

- **Airline Passenger Satisfaction Analysis**  
  Machine learning project for predicting passenger satisfaction and identifying the service factors that have the greatest influence on customer experience.

### 🌐 Software & Intelligent Systems

- **SwiftDrop – Flash Sale E-Commerce Platform**  
  High-performance PHP/MySQL platform designed to handle concurrent flash-sale purchases while preventing overselling using database transactions and locking mechanisms.

- **NEXLAB / SURAS – Smart University Resource Allocation System**  
  Intelligent resource-booking platform featuring priority scoring, conflict detection, waitlists, alternative scheduling, notifications, and administrative analytics.

- **Mentra / FocusZen – Smart Study Assistant**  
  Productivity platform with task management, Pomodoro sessions, study tracking, reminders, AI assistance, and community features.

### 🕸️ Research & Graph Analytics

- **ScholarGraph**  
  Graph-based analysis of research collaboration networks comparing **structural centrality, recent research productivity, and interdisciplinary breadth**.

- **News Story Propagation & Source Influence**  
  Graph-based research investigating how news stories propagate across media organizations and comparing structural influence with temporal story leadership.
